Booking & Deposits

A deposit is required to reserve individual Beauty By BLOW appointments.

Services under $150: $25 deposit

Services $150 and above: $50 deposit

Microblading: $50 deposit

Review the full service menu for current introductory and regular pricing before selecting your appointment.

Service Packages

Beauty By BLOW offers multi-session body and wellness packages for clients who want to build consistency into their self-care routine.

All service packages require payment in full.

Package pricing, included services, and session quantities are listed on the Services page.

The Studio

Beauty By BLOW is located in Buford, Georgia and operates from a private home studio. Appointments are designed to provide a private, personal, and unhurried experience with Asia Shari.

Address & Privacy

For the privacy and safety of Asia Shari's home studio, the full residential address is not published on this website.

Beauty By BLOW · Buford, GA · Private Home Studio

The full studio address will be shared directly with confirmed clients as part of their appointment details.

Consent & Safety

Depending on the service selected, clients may be asked to review and complete applicable intake, consent, safety, and aftercare information as part of the appointment process.

Please provide accurate information when completing any service-specific forms and review all preparation and aftercare instructions provided for your appointment.

If you have questions about whether a service is appropriate for you, contact an appropriate qualified healthcare professional before booking.
